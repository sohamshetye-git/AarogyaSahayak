# MoHFW Clinical Guidance - Respiratory Distress and Hypoxemia Management at PHC
Issuing Authority: Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW)
Document ID: DOC-MOHFW-RESP-004
Publication Date: 2024-01-05
Source: https://mohfw.gov.in/guidelines/respiratory_distress_phc.pdf

## Section 1: Assessment of Respiratory Compromise
Pulse oximetry is a mandatory vital sign evaluation for any patient presenting with cough, fever, or shortness of breath.
Classification of Hypoxemia:
- Normal: SpO2 95% - 100% on room air.
- Mild Hypoxemia: SpO2 93% - 94% requiring close clinical monitoring.
- Moderate to Severe Hypoxemia: SpO2 < 93% or SpO2 < 90% in children/pregnant women. Represents an acute respiratory emergency.

## Section 2: Clinical Warning Signs
- Tachypnea: Respiratory rate > 24 breaths/minute in adults (age-specific thresholds for pediatrics).
- Use of accessory respiratory muscles, intercostal retractions, or central cyanosis.
- Inability to speak in full sentences without pausing for breath.
- Altered mental status, confusion, or marked lethargy.

## Section 3: Primary Healthcare Interventions
1. Immediate Supplemental Oxygen: Administer high-flow oxygen via nasal cannula (2-4 L/min) or non-rebreather mask (10-15 L/min) targeting SpO2 >= 94% (88-92% in chronic COPD).
2. Bronchodilator Therapy: Administer nebulized Salbutamol (2.5 mg) + Ipratropium Bromide (500 mcg) if wheezing or bronchospasm is present.
3. Systemic Corticosteroids: Consider oral Dexamethasone 6 mg or Prednisolone 40 mg if acute severe asthma or COPD exacerbation.
4. Referral: Prompt transfer with continuous oxygen support to Higher Facility with ICU/Ventilatory capacity.
